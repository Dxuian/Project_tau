Mine BTC — 26 themes, one screen

Each panel is its own app. On a first visit it opens with a short tutorial on how Bitcoin mining works; this browser remembers once it's been seen, and "See tutorial" on the miner opens it again. Then it asks you to set up a wallet: create a new 12-word phrase (copy or download it) or upload a wallet / address. Then you can start and stop mining. Hashing runs in your browser as a demo and isn't connected to any pool. Tap ⤢ to open any panel full size.
